# Writing Style Analysis Dimensions

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Average sentence length and length distribution
- Proportion of simple, compound, and complex sentences
- Subordinate clause nesting depth and types (relative, adverbial, nominal)
- Rhythm of alternating long and short sentences

## 2. Vocabulary Features
- Academic vocabulary density (AWL coverage)
- Professional terminology frequency and distribution
- Ratio of abstract to concrete words
- Verb strength, preference for adjectives and adverbs
- Synonym substitution and lexical diversity (Type-Token Ratio)

## 3. Paragraph Organization
- Average paragraph length and length distribution
- Topic sentence position (beginning / middle / end)
- Internal development patterns (example, contrast, progression, classification)
- Cohesion and coherence between paragraphs

## 4. Argumentation Logic
- Use of deductive (general to specific) and inductive (specific to general) reasoning
- Hypothesis-test-conclusion structural patterns
- Presentation of causal chains (direct, multiple, interactive)
- Handling of refutation and concession

## 5. Tone and Register
- Formality level (continuum from casual to academic)
- Balance between subjectivity and objectivity
- Expression of authority, certainty, and humility (hedging language)
- Politeness strategies and reader awareness

## 6. Cohesion and Transition
- Density and types of logical connectors (addition, contrast, causation, time)
- Pronoun anaphora and lexical repetition strategies
- Inter-paragraph transition methods (summary-introduction, question-answer, parallel development)
- Information flow organization (given to new)

## 7. Citation and Intertextuality
- Citation density (citations per 1000 words)
- Ratio of direct quotation, indirect quotation, and paraphrase
- Integration of references (sentence-initial / medial / final)
- Evaluative language when citing (support, contrast, criticism)

## 8. Voice and Person
- Proportion and distribution of active and passive voice
- First-person (I/we) usage strategy and frequency
- Second-person (you) usage
- Impersonal structures (it is suggested that...)

## 9. Rhetoric and Expression
- Frequency and types of metaphor and analogy
- Rhetorical devices (parallelism, antithesis)
- Interactive strategies (rhetorical questions, leading questions)
- Emphasis devices (repetition, inversion, cleft sentences)

## 10. Data and Evidence Presentation
- Statistical description style (mean, significance, confidence intervals)
- Citation style for figures, tables, and formulas
- Detail and perspective of case narratives
- Connection between data and argumentation

## 11. Titles and Structure
- Chapter title naming style (descriptive vs. argumentative)
- Heading hierarchy usage habits
- Abstract writing mode (structured vs. narrative)
- Functional positioning of introduction and conclusion

## 12. Opening and Closing
- Opening methods (background introduction, problem posing, direct statement, data introduction)
- Closing methods (summary, future outlook, suggestion, echoing the opening)
- Use of transition paragraphs
